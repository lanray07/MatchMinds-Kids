A little help for grown-ups

How do we begin?

Tap Let's play, choose an explorer, open Woodland wonder and choose an included starter game. Tap two cards to find a pair. There is no forced timer.

Can we play without internet?

Included gameplay, local profiles and saved progress work offline. Purchases and restoration need the Amazon Appstore and a connection.

I cannot hear Milo

Check the device media volume, then Parents → Sound & voice. Spoken guidance can use an installed offline voice or packaged English instructions. All instructions also appear as text.

Where is the microphone button?

Commands are optional and off by default. They are unavailable until a reviewed build enables them and a compatible on-device recogniser is present. Many Fire tablets do not support this. Touch controls and narration remain usable.

How do I restore premium?

Use the purchasing Amazon account, connect to the internet, then open Parents → Premium & restore purchases → Restore purchases. A purchase is unlocked only after verification. Avoid purchasing again while a verification request is unresolved.

I forgot the parent PIN

There is no email recovery because the app has no account. Clearing app data in Android settings resets profiles, progress and settings. Afterwards restore purchases using the purchasing Amazon account. Keep the PIN private and avoid showing it to children.

Delete profiles or progress

Open the parent area, choose the relevant deletion control and confirm. The action cannot be undone. Deleting play data does not cancel purchases with Amazon.
