MatchMinds Kids privacy policy

Draft date: 15 October 2026. Publisher: [PUBLISHER]. Contact: [CONTACT EMAIL], [POSTAL CONTACT].

Play without an account

MatchMinds Kids does not ask children to register an account, supply a real name, date of birth, photo, location or contact details. There is no advertising, behavioural tracking, public sharing or chat.

Information on your device

Nicknames, selected avatars, difficulty preferences, settings, completed levels, stars and an unfinished game are stored in the app's private storage. Backup and device-transfer rules exclude this data. A salted parent-PIN hash is stored instead of the PIN itself.

Voice guidance and optional commands

Spoken guidance uses bundled English audio or an installed voice marked as usable offline. Optional microphone commands are disabled in the current default build. If enabled in a reviewed release, they require parental activation, microphone permission, a compatible on-device recogniser and an explicit tap for each command. The app does not write voice recordings or transcripts to storage and does not fall back to a cloud recogniser. Review the release-specific recogniser disclosure before enabling this feature. Touch controls remain available.

Purchases

Amazon handles payment. When purchasing or restoring premium, the app supplies the Amazon customer identifier and receipt identifier to [VERIFIER HOST], operated for [PUBLISHER] by [HOSTING PROVIDER], to verify the receipt with Amazon. No child nickname or gameplay record is attached. The service's retention and infrastructure logging policy is [RETENTION POLICY]. The app stores the resulting entitlement and purchasing Amazon identifier for offline access and account-change handling. The developer does not receive payment-card information.

Deleting information

A parent can remove individual profiles, clear play progress, or delete profiles and settings behind the parent PIN. The latter preserves the local premium entitlement and Amazon identifier; uninstalling or clearing app data removes all local app information. Purchases remain with Amazon and may be restored.
