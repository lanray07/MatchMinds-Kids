# MatchMinds Kids data deletion and support

Publisher: Lanray. Updated: 15 October 2026.

MatchMinds Kids has no child sign-in account. Explorer profiles and progress are stored on the device. You can remove play data without deleting an Amazon account.

## Delete local play data

1. Open **Parents** and enter your private parent PIN.
2. Open the profiles or progress controls and choose the relevant profile deletion, progress clearing or erase-all option.
3. Read the confirmation before choosing **Delete**. Deleted play records cannot be recovered through the app.

To remove all app-private storage, including parent settings and any local purchase-entitlement cache, use the device's application settings to clear MatchMinds Kids storage. Device menu names vary. This resets local profiles, progress and the parent PIN. The app excludes its data from Android backup and device transfer.

Removing local play data does not cancel an Amazon purchase, issue a refund or delete Amazon's transaction records. If premium purchase functionality is enabled in the version you use, restore access through the purchasing Amazon account after resetting storage.

## Ask for help or request deletion

An adult can email **[banksmi@mail.com](mailto:banksmi@mail.com?subject=MatchMinds%20Kids%20data%20deletion%20request)** with the subject **MatchMinds Kids data deletion request**. Say whether the request concerns local play data, a previous support message or purchase verification. Do not include a child's real name, voice recording, parent PIN, password or payment-card details. We will respond through that email conversation and explain any information needed to identify a relevant support or verification record.

The publisher cannot remotely access or erase local play records. For information held by Amazon, use Amazon's account privacy and customer-support controls. The current prerelease build has no active publisher purchase-verification service. Before one is enabled, its operator, hosting and retention details must be added to the [privacy policy](PRIVACY.md).

Support information may need to be retained to resolve a request or meet applicable obligations. Ask about any record you previously supplied and the reason for retaining it. This page does not promise deletion of Amazon records or records the publisher does not control.
